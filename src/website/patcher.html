Client tools · Ships in the source

Your APK, unpinned.

The Windstock Patcher is a desktop GUI that removes certificate pinning from a Pokémon GO APK, so the client trusts your own server's local CA. It's real, it's in the repo, and the whole thing is GPLv3.

It exists. It does
exactly one thing.

Point it at a Pokémon GO 0.35 APK and it strips the certificate pinning — that's the whole job. (The 0.29 build instead needs a one-time static metadata patch; the walkthroughs in the repo cover both.) No root, and your own CA is installed on the phone, not replaced.

Meanwhile, elsewhere

No launcher.
No signing ceremony.

Kanto's setup hands you their launcher and browser patcher, “gets the matching original app”, and on iPhone makes you sign and sideload the IPA with your own Apple ID. Windstock's flow is one script that deletes a pin. The fancier version is over there; the open version is here.
