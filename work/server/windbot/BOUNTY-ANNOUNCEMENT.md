# 🎬 The Windstock Video Bounty is LIVE!

Love Windstock? Show it off on **TikTok** and get paid in-game! 💰

**How it works**
1️⃣ Record a TikTok of you playing Windstock: a shiny catch, a gym takeover, the spooky Halloween nights, anything!
2️⃣ Put **windstock.org** in your caption
3️⃣ Link your trainer with `/link` (get the code on your windstock.org profile)
4️⃣ Send your video in with `/bounty link:<your TikTok link>`

**Rewards** 🏆
• **Just for posting**: 50 coins
• **1,000 views**: 150 coins + 2 Lucky Eggs
• **10,000 views**: 800 coins + 5 Incense + 3 Lucky Eggs
• **25,000 views**: 1,500 coins + 1 Master Ball + 2 Shiny Incense ✨

Rewards stack: a video that reaches 10k views earns the posting, 1k and 10k rewards!

**Best video of the month** 🥇 also wins a special prize picked by the team.

**Rules**
• Your own gameplay only. No reposted or stolen clips
• No bought views or bots. That gets you disqualified
• Views are checked about **7 days after posting**
• Up to 3 videos a day
• Rewards go to the trainer you linked, and you'll get a DM from WindBot when it pays out
• Please turn on TikTok's **"Branded content"** toggle, since you're getting a reward

Type `/bounty` with no link anytime to see the rules again.

Let's put Windstock on the map! 🌍
